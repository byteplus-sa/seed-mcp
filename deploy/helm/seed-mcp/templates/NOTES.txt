This is one SQLite writer, not an HA application. Two VKE nodes provide
same-zone rescheduling capacity only. Never scale the StatefulSet or force-delete
a pod on an unreachable node without proving the old writer is stopped.
Preserve the state PVC and the original HMAC key/version across restarts.
StorageClass and PVC retention do not replace tested backups.

Record version/readiness evidence privately. values.ci.yaml is synthetic and

custom provider headers leaking on proxy errors. Use safe app metrics for
operations; do not enable request logging as a troubleshooting shortcut.
